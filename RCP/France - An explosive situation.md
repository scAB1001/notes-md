#### Summary of the article
The article published on Ocotber 2nd is titled "France: An explosive situation" splitting the conversation into two relate parts: a worker movement and a student movement.
The worker side of things covers the organisation of mass strikes by a major French labour trade union, the CGT. This strike took place on the 8th of October in response to workers at TotalEnergies SE reaching a breaking point over pay, working conditions and more ---all while the company reports high profits and C-suit executives are paid handsomely.

By looking at [TotalEnergies' Annual Financial Report](https://totalenergies.com/investors/publications-and-regulated-information/regulated-information/annual-financial-reports) we can find the the ratios and trends between the level of compensation of the Chairman and Chief Executive Officer and the average/median compensation of TotalEnergies SE employees as per French Commercial Code.
